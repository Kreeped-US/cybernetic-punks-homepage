How we'd explain it on /about

What the badges mean. Every article on the network carries a provenance badge at the top. It answers one question before you read a word: where did this information come from, and how sure are we? We built the network on verified, primary-source data, and the badge is that commitment made visible on every page.

Why five tiers, not a score. A single number would flatten the one thing that matters — the difference between confirmed and reported. So instead of a rating, each article declares its evidence: Verified means every figure was checked against a primary source and confirmed in-game. Attributed means it came from the community and is credited as such, not confirmed by the developer. Analysis is our own read, built on verified data but labeled plainly as judgment, never dressed up as fact. Partial and Pending exist because honesty sometimes means saying "some of this is confirmed, some isn't — yet."

What it is not. The badge is never a marketing number and never a popularity score. It reflects evidence, not traffic. When a source changes, we correct the article and the badge moves with it. When we can't confirm something, we say so rather than fill the gap — the honest null is the point.
