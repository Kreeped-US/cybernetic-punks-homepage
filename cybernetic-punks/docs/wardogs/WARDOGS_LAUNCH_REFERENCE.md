## TIER 1 -- BULKHEAD-CONFIRMED (official Steam announcements / store page -- state as fact)

- LAUNCH: Wednesday, September 10, 2026, 16:00 UTC. Steam / PC (Windows) only. No invite, key, or
  queue -- open purchase at launch.
- PRICE: Standard $39.99; Supporter Edition $49.99. The Supporter Edition is COSMETIC ONLY (faction
  weapon camos, a helicopter paint job, a scoreboard tag/icon, bobbleheads). Regional pricing exists
  (e.g. some LatAm / Turkey tiers around $24.99).
- NO MICROTRANSACTIONS beyond the Supporter Edition are planned during Early Access (Bulkhead
  stated).
- 37 WEAPONS confirmed at EA launch (official Bulkhead Steam announcement, Aug 11 2026). Additional
  weapons are scheduled as post-launch content.
- 3 FREE ASSAULT RIFLES: A-91, Bushmaster M17S, KH-2002 -- the free faction recruit rifles. Note:
  the free guns have NO full-auto. Plus a free backpack with limited storage.
- BETA PROGRESS DOES NOT CARRY to EA -- clean slate at launch.
- IDENTITY: a 100-player, three-faction, large-scale tactical FPS. Three rival teams contest
  randomized Control Zones; combined arms (infantry + vehicles + destructible environments +
  building/fortification). Economy is buy-your-loadout-from-a-vendor (no fixed class/kit): cash
  carries between matches, and you buy a kit before each life.
- ROADMAP -- STATED DIRECTION, NOT LAUNCH FEATURES: fighter jets, expanded weapon categories, and
  new vehicle types are named as Early Access ROADMAP items, not launch content. Bulkhead framed
  post-launch as leaning toward bug-fixes / balance / community feedback rather than frequent new
  content. (Do NOT describe roadmap items as available at launch.)

## TIER 2 -- BETA-OBSERVED / COMMUNITY-RECORDED (NDA-lifted; OBSERVED, not official)

Label every item from this tier "recorded in beta/alpha, subject to launch changes." Do NOT state
as Bulkhead-official.

- CONCURRENCY: combined Closed Beta peak ~245,000 (244,926) across the two betas.
- SCOPE (widely reported; treat as expected, not official-confirmed, unless a Bulkhead source is
  found): ~21 vehicles and 3 maps (3 variations each) expected at launch.
- VENDOR ECONOMY (recorded in alpha/beta):
  - ~$10,000 one-time STARTING cash per account (not per match).
  - a -50% "new recruit" discount below roughly level 9.
  - ammunition billed SEPARATELY by caliber (~$10-$250 per box).
  - the ALPHA vendor roster was 33 weapons (one faction playable); beta observed ~35.
- VEHICLES (recorded in beta): transport and attack helicopters; a main battle tank (~$250k,
  level-gated); jeeps; a recon vehicle with a radar dome usable as a MOBILE SPAWN (team at 20 pts,
  roughly once per 10 minutes). ~20 vehicles counted in alpha.
- WEAPON CATEGORIES observed (9): assault rifles, SMGs, shotguns, LMGs, marksman rifles, sniper
  rifles, bows, sidearms, launchers. Attachments (optics / foregrips / mags) unlock via WARDOG
  Level -- not bought outright.

## TIER 3 -- CHANGES SINCE BETA (reported -- for a "what changed" piece; attribute as reported, confirm at launch)

- PROGRESSION SLOWED at launch vs beta (harder to unlock top-tier gear early).
- The TANK re-gated: moved from a Wardog-level-50 gate (beta) to a DRIVER-level gate (launch),
  making it slower to unlock.

## TIER 4 -- HONEST-NULL / UNKNOWN (do NOT invent)

- No official per-weapon PRICE LIST published by Bulkhead -- all prices are beta-recorded (Tier 2).
- No official full weapon LIST / named roster from Bulkhead -- "37" is a count, not a roster.
- Exact launch mechanics beyond 16:00 UTC (kernel anti-cheat, Steam Deck / Proton status,
  crossplay) -- unconfirmed / unaddressed.
- Map NAMES / POIs beyond the counts -- not officially detailed.

## Writing rules for launch-week articles

- State Tier 1 as fact, sourced to Bulkhead's official announcement / store page.
- Tier 2: label "recorded in the beta/alpha, subject to change at launch" -- never "Bulkhead
  confirms." Concurrency, vehicle/map counts, and all prices are Tier 2 until live EA verifies them.
- Tier 3: "reported to have changed since the beta" -- confirm in-game at launch before restating
  as fact.
- Tier 4: leave unstated. Do not fill a gap with a community guess presented as the answer.
- The 37-vs-33 gap: 37 is the confirmed launch count (Tier 1); 33 is the observed structured roster
  (Tier 2). State both honestly; never imply 33 is the full launch arsenal.

CHANGE DISCIPLINE: update from a new primary source (a Bulkhead announcement / store change, or --
post-launch -- live in-game verification). The post-launch pass promotes Tier-2 items that live EA
confirms into Tier 1 (with the source), and moves anything the launch build contradicts into a
corrections note. Keep the sourcing line and the LAUNCH-DATED note current.
